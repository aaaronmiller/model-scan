## Formula

- Per benchmark: independent results win (best configuration per source, median across sources); publisher-only values are multiplied by 0.85 and count 0.75 of their weight.
- Normalisation: minmax over the ranked pool. Each family's weighted mean is shrunk toward 0.2 by 2.0 units of pseudo-evidence, so missing benchmarks are not zeros.
- Families mixed as {"agentic coding": 0.6, "repository repair": 0.15, "general agent": 0.25}; context adds up to 0.03 on a log scale from 32768 to 1048576 tokens.
- Tiers: share of the top score, minimum evidence and tool support, from `config.json`.
