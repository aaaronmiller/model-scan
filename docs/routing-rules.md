# ARDF routing rules of thumb

Generated 2026-11-17 by `calibrate.py` from `sources/current/` (AA snapshot 2026-11-10). Do not edit by hand.

Short list. Every rule cites the data behind it. Scores are the AA v4.2 composite recomputed by ARDF; costs are AA workload costs per task in USD, not subscription bills. Starting hypotheses, not guarantees: verify availability, quota and terms at dispatch, and check a rule on your own task before relying on it.

1. **Choose from the frontier first; a dominated configuration has a cheaper or equal and at least as good alternative.**  
   Data: 22 of 109 scored configurations with a trusted cost are undominated (21 of 108 when only prices above $0 count); 2 scored configurations with an unverified AA price of $0 are held out of both (full lists: docs/full-frontier.md). Example: Claude Fable 5.1 (Max, Default Fallback) (49.09, $12.99) is dominated by GPT-6.1 Sol (High) (50.31, $0.591).
2. **To reach a target score, start with the cheapest configuration that clears it.**  
   Data: Cheapest priced configuration at or above each score: 20: GPT-6.1 Terra (Low) $0.0713; 30: Muse Ember (Xhigh) $0.116; 40: GPT-6.1 Terra (High) $0.259; 50: GPT-6.1 Sol (High) $0.591; 55: GPT-6 Astra (Xhigh) $3.66; 60: Gemini 4 Carbon (High) $7.76.
3. **Each extra point at the top costs far more than at the bottom; buy the top tier only when the task needs it.**  
   Data: Cheapest config at 40 or more is $0.259; at 60 or more it is $7.76, 29.9x as much (GPT-6.1 Terra (High) versus Gemini 4 Carbon (High)).
4. **Treat max effort as an exception: price it against xhigh on your task before using it.**  
   Data: Across 10 families with both settings, max costs a median 1.66x xhigh (range 1.17x to 2.45x) for a median +2.01 points (range -0.78 to +6.04). Max scored lower than xhigh in 1 of 10: Claude Fable 5.1 (Max, Default Fallback) scores 49.09 against Claude Fable 5.1 (Xhigh, Default Fallback) 49.87 (-0.78) at 1.20x cost. 3 of 10 max configurations are on the frontier.
5. **A higher effort setting does not always score higher: compare the step you are buying inside the same model before paying for it.**  
   Data: Across 55 model families, 53 adjacent effort steps (for example low to medium); the higher setting scored lower in 2: GPT-6.1 Terra (Low) 29.42 against GPT-6.1 Terra (Non-reasoning) 30.50 (-1.08, 0.32x cost); Claude Fable 5.1 (Max, Default Fallback) 49.09 against Claude Fable 5.1 (Xhigh, Default Fallback) 49.87 (-0.78, 1.20x cost). Context only, and not evidence of non-monotonicity because it compares different models: frontier members by effort are none 1, low 3, medium 5, high 5, xhigh 5, max 3 of 22 (minimal appear on no frontier row).
6. **Treat default-fallback tracks as their own configurations, not as the standalone model; the pinned data does not say what the fallback covers.**  
   Data: AA lists 21 fallback tracks, 20 scored (Anthropic 20) and 1 unscored (Claude Fable 5 (Max, Opus 4.8 Fallback)); none of them has a scored standard-track counterpart, so none can be compared with a standard track. Fallback tracks are 5 of 22 frontier members (Anthropic 5 of 6). Without them the frontier has 18 members (fallback-free frontier in docs/full-frontier.md), and every fallback track is tagged in that document.
7. **No single provider holds the whole frontier: before locking to one, check that it is on the frontier at the score you need.**  
   Data: Frontier members by provider, with the lowest and highest score of those members (the span between first and last member, not a guarantee that every score in between is covered): OpenAI 11 (9.67 to 55.30); Anthropic 6 (18.64 to 64.56, 5 fallback tracks; every member above 18.64 is a fallback track); Google 2 (58.28 to 60.02); Meta 2 (34.11 to 36.02); Cohere 1 (3.42 to 3.42). Frontier score range overall: 3.42 to 64.56. Each provider's own frontier is in docs/full-frontier.md.
8. **Do not trust an AA price of $0 until a free route with the same name is listed; unverified $0 configurations stay out of the frontier, and a verified one still needs its rate limits and terms checked.**  
   Data: AA lists 3 scored $0 configurations. Verified free route: North Mini Code 3.42 (free route listed: OpenRouter cohere/north-mini-code:free; Kilo cohere/north-mini-code:free). Unverified, held out of dominance: K2 Horizon 375B A23B 17.73 (AA lists $0 and no route with this name exists in the pinned OpenRouter, Kilo or OpenCode Zen catalogs); Command A+ 6.24 (AA lists $0 but the catalogs list a paid route: OpenRouter cohere/command-a-plus $0.3/M in, $1.5/M out; Kilo cohere/command-a-plus $0.3/M in, $1.5/M out). For scale, the best of all three (17.73) is matched by a paid configuration at $0.0422 (GPT-6 Luna (Medium)).
9. **Use free routes for bounded, verifiable work; their scores are ceilings or assumptions, and even the ceiling is well below the paid frontier.**  
   Data: 8 free-route models have a measured score (39 free catalog entries in total); 3 are scored at an effort their route lists and 5 on the assumption that the route runs AA's configuration. Highest: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max) at 32.59 (identity unverified), a ceiling: it is the score at max, the highest effort its route lists that AA measured, with a floor of 18.51; the catalog states no default, so default use can score lower; that is at most 50% of the top frontier score 64.56. The cheapest paid configuration at or above it is Muse Ember (Xhigh) at $0.116 (34.11). See docs/free-routes.md.
10. **Never mix cost bases: Priors subscription-equivalent costs sit below API workload costs by a lab-dependent factor.**  
   Data: Median Priors API to subscription cost ratio by lab: Anthropic 56.6x, Mistral 21.0x, OpenAI 18.5x, Alibaba 13.7x, xAI 10.8x, Google 2.8x, Meta 1.0x, Other 1.0x. ARDF costs are API workload costs.
11. **Do not read ARDF and Priors agreeing as validation, and keep each comparison within one source and one cost basis.**  
   Data: The agreement is circular: ARDF's composite uses the Priors weights (15/5/10/15/10/5/5/10/10/15) on the same AA snapshot, so on 93 of 100 Priors API rows the scores differ by a median 0.0025 and at most 0.0050 points (Priors' two-decimal rounding) and API cost by at most 0.0039 USD per task by construction. AA's own published index, which does not share those weights, differs from the ARDF composite on 116 configurations by a median 0.0202 and at most 0.2824 points, with 15 above 0.1 (largest: Qwen3.8 Max (0902), -0.2824). Exact tables: docs/priors-deltas.md.
12. **No score is not a low score; it means the benchmark set is incomplete.**  
   Data: 618 of 734 primary configurations have no complete 10-benchmark score and are not ranked; they are listed on the dashboard's unscored shelf.
